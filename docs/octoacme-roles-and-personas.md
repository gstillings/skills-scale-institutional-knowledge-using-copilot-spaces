# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## UX Designer

### Role Summary
UX Designers design user experiences and interfaces that align with customer needs and business objectives. They ensure products are intuitive, accessible, and delightful to use.

### Responsibilities
- Conduct user research and usability testing
- Create wireframes, prototypes, and design specifications
- Collaborate with Product Managers on requirements definition
- Work with Developers throughout the implementation lifecycle
- Validate designs through user feedback and testing
- Maintain design systems and component libraries

### Goals
- Deliver user-centered design solutions that solve real problems
- Ensure consistent and accessible user experiences
- Reduce friction and improve user satisfaction
- Validate design decisions with data and user feedback

### Collaboration
- Works closely with Product Managers to understand user needs and business goals
- Collaborates with Developers to ensure designs are technically feasible and properly implemented
- Coordinates with Project Managers on design milestones and deliverables
- Integrates feedback from Support and customer-facing teams

---

## DevOps Engineer

### Role Summary
DevOps Engineers manage CI/CD pipelines, infrastructure, and deployment processes to enable reliable and efficient releases. They bridge development and operations to support continuous delivery.

### Responsibilities
- Maintain and optimize CI/CD pipelines and automation
- Automate infrastructure provisioning and configuration
- Monitor deployments, system health, and performance
- Support incident response and troubleshooting
- Implement infrastructure as code and security best practices
- Coordinate release activities and rollback procedures

### Goals
- Enable fast, reliable, and repeatable deployments
- Minimize deployment-related incidents and downtime
- Improve developer productivity through automation
- Maintain system reliability and observability

### Collaboration
- Works with Developers to integrate automation and improve build processes
- Coordinates with Project Managers on release schedules and rollback plans
- Collaborates with Security Lead on secure deployment practices
- Partners with Support Lead on incident response and system monitoring

---

## Security Lead

### Role Summary
Security Leads oversee security best practices throughout the development lifecycle and operational processes. They ensure security is integrated into planning, development, and release activities.

### Responsibilities
- Conduct threat modeling and security risk assessments
- Review code and architecture for security vulnerabilities
- Advise on secure coding practices and standards
- Coordinate security testing and vulnerability scanning
- Lead incident response for security issues
- Maintain security policies and compliance documentation

### Goals
- Identify and mitigate security risks early in development
- Ensure compliance with security standards and regulations
- Build security awareness across the team
- Minimize security incidents and vulnerabilities in production

### Collaboration
- Works with all roles to integrate security into planning, development, and release
- Collaborates with Developers on secure code reviews and remediation
- Partners with DevOps Engineers on secure infrastructure and deployment
- Coordinates with Project Managers on security milestones and risk management

---

## Support Lead

### Role Summary
Support Leads coordinate post-release customer support and issue triage. They serve as the voice of the customer and ensure timely resolution of customer problems.

### Responsibilities
- Track and triage inbound support issues and escalations
- Communicate customer feedback and trends to Product and Project teams
- Monitor issue resolution and customer satisfaction
- Maintain support documentation and knowledge base
- Coordinate bug fixes and support-driven improvements
- Provide customer-facing communications during incidents

### Goals
- Deliver timely and effective customer support
- Reduce time to resolution for customer issues
- Identify and communicate product improvement opportunities
- Maintain high customer satisfaction scores

### Collaboration
- Works with Developers on bug fixes and technical troubleshooting
- Partners with Product Managers to prioritize support-driven improvements
- Coordinates with Project Managers on issue tracking and escalations
- Provides feedback to UX Designers on usability issues and customer pain points

## How these personas are used in the exercise
- Use these persona definitions to frame scenarios and sample interactions in the Skills Exercise.
- Each persona can be used as a persona prompt for Copilot Spaces to shape role-specific guidance.
- When initiating projects, consider which roles are relevant and ensure their responsibilities are addressed.
