# OctoAcme Project Management Framework

## Overview

OctoAcme follows a structured yet flexible project management framework designed to deliver customer value through iterative, cross-functional collaboration. Our approach is built on five core phases: **Initiation**, **Planning**, **Execution**, **Release**, and **Retrospective**. During Initiation, we validate business needs through a Project One-pager that defines the problem, success metrics, stakeholder alignment, and resource requirements. Planning establishes detailed scope, timelines, milestones, and dependencies, ensuring teams have clear roadmaps and acceptance criteria. Execution focuses on day-to-day delivery using agile workflows with daily standups, weekly syncs, and regular demos to maintain momentum and transparency. Release encompasses deployment, verification, and stakeholder communication to ensure smooth production rollouts. Finally, Retrospective sessions capture learnings and convert them into actionable improvements, fostering a culture of continuous enhancement.

## Roles and Responsibilities

Three primary roles drive project success at OctoAcme: **Project Managers (PM)**, **Product Managers (PdM)**, and **Developers**, supported by QA/Testing and Stakeholders. Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications, maintaining transparency across all stakeholders through weekly status updates, risk registers, and decision logs. Product Managers define what should be built to maximize customer and business value, owning the product vision, prioritizing the backlog, and measuring outcomes through data-driven decisions. Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components, participating in code reviews, writing tests and documentation, and helping identify technical risks. QA/Testing validates quality and acceptance criteria through unit tests, integration tests, end-to-end smoke tests, and manual feature acceptance testing. This clear ownership model ensures accountability while promoting psychological safety and collaborative problem-solving.

## Communication and Risk Management

OctoAcme maintains consistent communication rhythms to keep teams aligned and risks visible. Core cadences include weekly syncs between PM and PdM, twice-weekly standups for delivery teams, monthly stakeholder updates, and ad-hoc escalations as needed. Our risk management approach uses a simple Risk Register that tracks each risk's impact, likelihood, owner, mitigation plan, and status—reviewed and updated during weekly syncs. Communication follows structured templates for weekly status updates (progress, next steps, risks/blockers, decisions needed) and incident responses (triage summary, actions, timeline, post-incident retrospective). Escalation paths are clearly defined from team-level to PM to Product Lead to Sponsor, with specialized protocols for security incidents. All project information is maintained in a single source of truth, typically the project README or release documentation, ensuring stakeholders always have access to current status and decisions.

## Integrated Quality Assurance

Quality assurance is embedded throughout the OctoAcme development lifecycle rather than treated as a separate phase. During execution, teams follow pull request workflows with small PRs (≤400 lines when possible), automated testing and linting in CI, and required code review approvals before merging. Testing strategies include unit tests for new logic, integration tests where applicable, end-to-end smoke tests for critical flows before release, and security scanning in CI. Project boards (e.g., GitHub Projects) track work through defined columns from Backlog to Done, with QA as an explicit stage. Teams monitor success metrics, velocity, burndown, and key signals like errors, latency, and usage through dashboards. Retrospectives regularly examine quality outcomes and process effectiveness, generating prioritized action items with clear owners and due dates. This integrated approach ensures quality is built into every step, enabling rapid iteration while maintaining reliability and security standards.
